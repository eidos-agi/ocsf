# OCSF Examples

These are **fictionalized / simplified** packs for demonstrating the format.

They use publicly known high-level structures of major companies for realism, but are **not** official, complete, or authoritative records of any real company.

Never treat them as legal or financial source of truth.

## Available examples

- `ford-motor-group-simplified/` — Simplified illustrative pack inspired by the public corporate architecture of Ford Motor Company (dual-class voting, major subsidiaries, brands, credit arm, etc.).
