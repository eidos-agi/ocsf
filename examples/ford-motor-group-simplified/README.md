# Ford Motor Group — Simplified Illustrative Example

This pack is a **teaching / demonstration** example inspired by the publicly disclosed corporate structure of Ford Motor Company.

## What it shows

- Parent: Ford Motor Company (Delaware)
- Key wholly-owned subsidiaries:
  - Ford Motor Credit Company LLC (finance arm)
  - Ford Motor Company of Canada, Limited
  - Ford-Werke GmbH (Germany)
  - Ford Motor Company Limited (UK)
  - Ford Global Technologies, LLC (IP)
  - BlueOval Battery Michigan, LLC
- Brands: Ford + Lincoln
- Dual-class voting note (Class B / Ford family control)

## Important disclaimer

- This is **not** an official or complete record of Ford Motor Company.
- Ownership percentages, instrument details, and many intermediate entities are simplified or omitted.
- Data is derived only from public filings (primarily SEC Exhibit 21 and 10-K language).
- Do **not** use this pack for legal, tax, regulatory, or investment purposes.

## Source references (public)

- Ford Motor Company Form 10-K and Exhibit 21 (Subsidiaries of Ford Motor Company)
- Public proxy / ownership disclosures regarding Class B shares and voting power
