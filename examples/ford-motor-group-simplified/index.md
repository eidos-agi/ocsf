# Ford Motor Group (Simplified Illustrative)

This pack demonstrates how OCSF models a real-world multi-entity automotive group with:

- A public parent with dual-class share structure
- Wholly-owned operating and finance subsidiaries
- Brand ownership (Ford + Lincoln)
- International entities
- Joint-venture interests

**Important**: This is a teaching / demonstration pack only. Percentages, exact ownership chains, and instrument details are simplified or omitted where public data does not cleanly map. Do not use for legal, tax, or investment decisions.
